Kingdom of Graodom

General Notes

Political Alignment : Centre-Right, leaning Authoritarian

King, Queen or Ruler : King Regem Vita, 

Democratic or Dictatorship : Democratic with approval from a Monarchy

Main Trade : Everything

Capital : Beastead

List of Royal Family : King Ignis Regem Rex, Queen Pretio Dedit Rex (deceased, died during birth) and Prince Ignar Detis Rex (De-facto Leader of the Council of Graodom)

Military Power (1/10) : 9

Armies / Enforcement: The King has his Private King’s Order, as for the general public, they have the Royal Guards in the Capital and localized enforcement by Town’s / City’s Guards

General Environment : Lots of Mountain Ranges, especially in the center but apart from that primarily consists of regular plains and forests.

Detailed Notes

Environment

With the Kingdom of Graodom being where the extinction point was, the most high value alcohol is pre-extinction Wine, going for hundreds of gold pieces. Besides that, a common drink for most people is House Wine, Brandy and Cider from techniques developed by the various countries annexed into Graodom. Thankfully, due to their diplomatic positions internationally, they are able to import even expensive alcohols from other countries, such as Rhiaal and Rotterlund. 

History

Often called the After the fall of the Lich King, a grand total of thirteen small nations were made within the confines of what-would-be-now Graodom. At odds against one another, aiming to compete and be the best, which resulted in most of these countries engaging in wars with neighboring countries. This was until a bedazzling High-Elf by the name Ignis Regem Rex started to enchant people with his words, with people becoming enthralled by his sheer presence. Through diplomatic unions, Ignis Regem Rex had amassed the title “The Great King from Beyond” for his otherworldly charm.

After snowballing what would soon to be an empire just off diplomatic discussions, he very quickly found himself at the helm of a great kingdom. Having aspirations of his own, he allowed for a democracy to dictate the kingdom. But these members of the Council would need King Ignis’ approval before being allowed.

With the people’s first democratic votes to recruit Council Members, they ended up voting in a fair number of people with the Prince being a clear contender for the next election. With the Council of Ten, they decide the fate of Graodom, with Prince Ignar Detis Rex being the untitled de-facto Ruler of the Council due to his sway. Not much is known about the Prince since he is a very private person, however he similarly possesses his father's otherworldly charm.

Education and Academia

Ages 0-18 is home-schooling or tutors. 18+ is for advanced academics (such as Minas Academy, Apprenticeships or Private Tutoring)

Alcohol

With the Kingdom of Graodom being where the extinction point was, the most high value alcohol is pre-extinction Wine, going for hundreds of gold pieces a drop with some bottles being worth small castles. Besides that, a common drink for most people is House Wine, Mead and Cider from techniques developed by the various countries annexed into Graodom. Thankfully, due to their diplomatic positions internationally, they are able to import even expensive alcohols from other countries, such as Rhiaal and Rotterlund. 

Languages

With Graodom being a very diverse place, most of the mountain dwellers simply moved to Rotterlund to feel more at home there. Resulting in the minority of races and languages to be Dwarven, Gnomish and Halfling. Most of the population follow normal social norms.

Royal Laws

1 | No Murder of another person that is unjust in the eyes of Temida. Punishable by 10 years in Solitude.
2 | Tax is yearly, with it being 12% of Annual Pay, 10% goes to the The Democratic Council of Graodom and 2% goes to the Crown.
3 | Slavery is outlawed; you may discuss a consensual contract lasting up to 2 years which has to be officiated by an Official.
4 | Robbing a Vendor results in a week per 10GP worth of value was stolen up to 10,000GP. If It exceeds this amount, they will lose one hand and receive a brand on their stump, marking them a thief. 
5 |As per the Rotterlund/Graodom Accord, practice of Necromancy Magic outside of Healing Magic are forbidden.
6 | Investigations are to be conducted in three levels; first is Town/Village/City Court, second is Democratic Court and thirdly High Court. 

Town/Village/City Court is for small crimes such as theft under 100GP and general disorganized crime. Adventurers are free to come and assist with such cases. A closed jury of ten random people of the town alongside three guards will decide their punishment.

Democratic Court Level is for bigger scale crimes such as corruption, theft under 1,000GP, Murder and organized crime. Adventurers are requested to attain Investigation Permits and Signed Documentation from the respective Captain of the Town. A Jury consisting of investigators, guards and a captain (depending on the severity) are to pass judgement.

High Court Level is for treasonous crimes such as plots to overthrow royalty, theft under 100,000GP. Adventurers are required to attain a Royal Decree of Investigative Immunity signed by the entire Democratic Council of Graodom, The West and East Generals or The King himself. Only the The Democratic Council of Graodom may decide their punishment, the King can only issue a punishment if the crime committed was Treason or Extreme Blasphemy/Worship of Bhaal.

Democratic Laws

1 | Electoral Integrity and Candidacy - All prospective candidates for the Council of Ten, as well as local mayoralties, must undergo a formal vetting process by the current Council to ensure their platforms align with the stability of Graodom. Furthermore, the use of Enchantment or Charm magic to influence voters or political rivals is strictly prohibited and constitutes electoral treason.
2 | Guild and Commerce Regulation - As the trade capital of the world, all merchant guilds, artisan collectives, and private enterprises must register with the Council’s Bureau of Commerce. Monopolies are heavily restricted and can only operate if granted a special "Crown & Council Economic Charter."
3 | Public Assembly and Order - To maintain the peace established by the King, public demonstrations, protests, or unsanctioned gatherings exceeding thirty individuals require a permit issued by the local Democratic Court at least one week in advance. Unauthorized gatherings will be dispersed by the City Guards.
4 | Adventurer and Mercenary Oversight - Independent contractors, adventurers, and mercenary bands operating within Graodom must register for an official Guild License. Any dungeon wealth, bounties, or ancient artifacts recovered within Graodom’s borders must be declared to the Council for proper assessment of the 5% Royal Tax.
5 | Regional Autonomy and Zoning - Recognizing the cultural divide of the Kingdom, Western agricultural zones and Eastern metropolitan sectors are granted the right to establish local economic bylaws. However, these local laws cannot contradict Royal Laws, Council mandates, or the established religious exemptions of the Hallowed City of Eden and Dragon's Nest.
6 | Academic and Arcane Export - Given the preservation of knowledge at Minas Academy, the export of highly volatile magical artifacts, technologies of the First Men, or restricted arcane texts across Graodom’s borders requires a written permit signed by at least three members of the Council of Ten.
7 | Infrastructure and Expansion - Any new construction, excavation, or expansion of settlements into the central mountain ranges must be approved by the Council to ensure it does not disrupt established trade routes or unearth dangerous First Man ruins.

Supplementary Laws

Public Order and Safety

  • The Midnight Curfew: To prevent disorganized crime, citizens and visitors must be indoors or within a licensed tavern by the ringing of the midnight bell. Anyone walking the streets after hours must carry a lit lantern and a valid night-pass.
  • Peace-Bonding of Weapons: While citizens and adventurers may carry weapons, all bladed weapons must be peace-bonded (tied into their sheaths) while within the city walls or market districts. Unsheathing a weapon without cause is treated as a public disturbance.
  • Vagrancy and Loitering: Anyone roughing it in the town for more than three days must prove they have gainful employment, a registered guild contract, or sufficient coin to afford housing. "Frequent rough sleeping" in alleys or public squares is punishable by forced labor or exile from the town limits.

Commerce and Trade

  • Gate and Bridge Tolls: A local toll of 5 gold for each trading vehicle and an additional 10 gold for each vehicle after is required to enter the town gates or cross the lord's bridge, funding local guard salaries and road maintenance.
  • Market Stall Licensing: No goods may be sold on the streets without a local vendor's permit. Selling counterfeit, spoiled, or unlicensed goods results in the confiscation of the cart and a fine payable to the local lord.
  • The Guild Mandate: Any artisan (blacksmith, alchemist, brewer) operating within the jurisdiction must pay dues to the local chapter of their respective guild to ensure quality control and prevent undercutting local economies.

Resource Management and Land

  • The Lord's Protection: Hunting deer or boar, or logging for timber in designated "Lord's Forests" is strictly prohibited without a purchased writ. Gathering fallen branches or foraging for mushrooms/berries is permitted for the working class. These lands are often considered protected lands and are outside of the reach of the Democratic Council of Graodom as per the King's demands. The governing leader assigned to protect the land is responsible, however criminal activity within an area with the Lord's Protection still has to go through standard channels for sake of transparency and knowledge.
  • Livestock Zoning: No livestock larger than a dog may be kept within the inner city walls. All cattle, sheep, and large mounts must be stabled or grazed in the designated outer agricultural ring.
  • Water Rights: Diverting water from the main river for private agricultural or industrial use requires a permit. Dumping waste from tanneries, butcheries, or alchemy shops into the upstream water supply is heavily fined.

Magic and Adventurer Oversight

  • Arcane Disturbance Ordinance: Casting evocation or illusion magic within the town square that causes panic, property damage, or disruption of trade is punishable by a heavy fine or time in the local stocks.
  • Bounty Registration: Adventurers seeking to hunt monsters or bandits within the local lord's territory must register their intent with the local guard captain. This prevents mercenaries from claiming "bounties" on innocent locals or disrupting local guard investigations.

Unique Traditions and Religion

  • 19th - 26th of September is a time of celebration for the King and Prince's Birthday and the Late-Queen's Death-day, most shops will have sales on so they can close shop and go party since both the Council of Graodom and Royal Family will donate gold, alcohol and food for festivities.
  • In terms of population density, this place is easily the lowest despite having the highest population; many humble farmers live in the west of Graodom with some unmarked villages even cropping up here and there.
  • The Kingdom of Graodom was founded Temida's ethos as a fundamental basis. As a result, the Church of Temida coincide with the Crown and act independently of the The Democratic Council of Graodom.
  • Due to the history of the Kingdom of Graodom, the lands and the traditions there-in can vastly differ from place to place. In the West, people are more modest and humble, living quiet lives surrounded by monuments and architecture predating the Lich King. In the East, people live more grandiose lives, chasing dreams and high-paying jobs.
  • Two places are given religious exemption from The Democratic Council of Graodom, being the Hallowed City of Eden and Dragon's Nest. Due to the religious nature surrounding these places, they are granted the Protection of the Crown and operate under their own rules/laws local to the land.

  • Technological Advancements 1-10 / 6 - Thanks to Minas Academy having preserved lots of architectural information and countless schematics, they were able to reverse engineer most of the knowledge lost to time.
