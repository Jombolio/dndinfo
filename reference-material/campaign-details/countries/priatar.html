History

The various elders once owned their own land which was divided unfairly and often squabbled over amongst the land and who's family land belonged to what race often leading to violence and discrimination. The Elves of Rhiaal would ask for main-land territory for resources promptly resulting in the elders collectively saying "no" resulting in the ensuing war.

Alcohol

With the Kingdom of Graodom being where the extinction point was, the most high value alcohol is pre-extinction Wine, going for hundreds of gold pieces. Besides that, a common drink for most people is House Wine, Brandy and Cider from techniques developed by the various countries annexed into Graodom. Thankfully, due to their diplomatic positions internationally, they are able to import even expensive alcohols from other countries, such as Rhiaal and Rotterlund. 

Languages

Priaatar is the most diverse melting pot of languages, most citizens often speak up to six languages due to the various beastial races that work together. Besides that, Primordial and Sylvan are common alternatives for people to learn. Common is also commonly spoken due to frequent trade with the Kingdom of Graodom and Rotterlund

Laws

1 | You have the freedom afforded by free-speech and allows you espouse religious, philosophical and political beliefs. This also allows others to criticise you freely.

2 | Tax is yearly, with it being 10% of Annual Pay.

3 | Crimes are investigated by Certified Investigators, Tortles (due to their neutrality among the council) and members of the CoBTE.

4 | A small jury of local people from different walks of lives decide based on evidence present

5 | Should the jury deem the crime too serious for them, they can pass this up to the CoBTE

Traditions

  • There are very diverse food palettes, ranging from the Tortles whom are mostly vegetarian to the Leonin's whom are mostly carnivorous.
  • They are extremely distrusting of Elves. They are warmer towards Half-Elves since they are aware of their status within Rhiaal but are still slightly suspicious of them.
  • There would be diverse schools of thought ranging from Race Separatists of Loxodons all the way to the Dragonborn's way of Collectivism. Democracy is in full-swing.
  • Religious diversity is rather accepted!

Religion

  • Predominant: Bahamut and Sol
  • Uncommon: Anubis and Isis
  • Very Rare: Zeus,

  • Technological Advancements 6/10 - Since they have shared most of their wisdom handed down by tribes, it allowed them to at least advance their basic technology and infrastructure to a point of cohesion.

Class Values

  • The Working Class values Nature, Strength and Archery.

  • The Middle Class values Magic, Trading and Luxury Items.

  • The Upper Class values influence in the Council, Intellect and Resolve.
