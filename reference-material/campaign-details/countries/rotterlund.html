Rotterlund

General Notes

Political Alignment: Proto-Nationalist

King, Queen or Ruler: King Mondoe

Democratic or Dictatorship: Dictatorship

Main Trade: Raw Rare Materials such as Mithril and Adamantite, Commissioned Engineering and Electrical Consultation.

Capital: The Castle of Storms

List of Royal Family: King Mondo II, Queen Philandi I, Princess Yammi I

Military Power (1/10): 6

Armies / Enforcement:

  • Rotterlund Royal guards in the Castle of Storms
  • Citadel uses private security formed of in-house mages
  • The Lord's Guards such as the Three-Housed Guards of Barad Series or Greywood Guards.

General Environment: Full of Forests and Valleys, full of greenery. Only snowy in the Northern parts.

Detailed Notes

History

When the Lich King fell, a group of rowdy outlaws took to the mountains and fighting local criminals. After a few years, their reputation had proceeded them; they had encouraged a culture of "getting good", being good not just in body but in mind too.

As time went on, more and more people demanding he take up the little of King of the Lands. With his retainers support, he eventually took the throne.

However, he was an awful leader; policies never passed and all that was created was a culture of strength and adapting to surroundings. During this time, a daring lone man would attempt to beat the King at his own game, battle.

Beating the King, the new ruler took the throne and helped enact policies that help people and assist in rebuilding the areas mistreated by the previous King's retainers.

No one knows what happened to that of the old King.

Alcohol

Almost exclusively whiskey and rum is drank here due to the hearty natures of the drinks. Many popular whiskey brands around the world often stem from the Castle of Storms.

Languages

Thanks to the exploits of the previous King and retainers, some languages went extinct. Common is still predominant, with Dwarven, Halfling and Infernal being the three big alternatives. Some also speak Draconic and Undercommon additionally. It is surprisingly common to come across those whom can't read or write here.

King's Laws

1 | No mistreatment of another that is unjust in the eyes of Temida.
2 | Tax is yearly, with it being 20% of Annual Pay. 30% for Nobility.
3 | Slavery is outlawed; you may discuss a consensual contract lasting up to 2 years which has to be officiated by an Notary.
4 | Religion is to be only practiced at designated temples, lest being arrested for being a Public Nuisance. 
5 | As per the Rotterlund/Graodom Accord, practice of Necromancy outside of Healing uses are forbidden. Resurrection / Bringing Back the Dead is punishable by Death.
6 | In addition to these laws, local lords can enforce their own additional laws within their jurisdiction, so long they don't contradict laws above.

Religion

  • Predominant: Artemis and Temida
  • Uncommon: Hephaistos and Dionysus
  • Very Rare: Ares and Persephone

Traditions

  • Despite sometimes poor conditions, most are prideful about the country and often place heavy blame on local lords.
  • Mandatory Service is required for at least 12 months with the Local Guards to build core strength.
  • People from Rotterlund have a tendency to stereotype people based on looks and make assumptions quickly.
  • Whenever a new Lord is anointed and Knights are appointed, there is always a party in the Castle of Storms. The King often does not attend these however his Council mostly do.
  • Due to the events in Year 305 - The Great Necromancer of Gaeta Valley, Rotterlund has the lowest population of all of the developed nations. The events also Cursed the surrounding area, even seasoned Veteran's are encouraged not to explore.
  • The Royal Guard are known to be some of the strongest soldiers that the King personally recruits. Most working class people often strive to attempt to become Royal Guards both for the prestige of strength it meant as well as the lucrative yearly salary and benefits.

Technological, Divine and Arcane Advancements

  • Arcane Advancements 1-10 / 6 - Has the Citadel for documented advancements, but is meant for more academic and archival purposes rather than progressing Arcane studies.
  • Technological Advancements 1-10 / 9 - Due to the founding Dwarf's being naturally gifted when it comes to martial arts, they are natural iron-workers and engineers, combine that with the constant landfalls around them, they became architectural masters. This has led to an overt reliance on engineering and artificery.
  • Divine Advancements 1-10 / 3 - Isn’t much of a monotheist country but the shackles of Private Practice can easily lead to secret radicalization and privatization of divine knowledge.

Class Values

  • The Working Class values familial relations, honest living and good meals.
  • The Middle Class values Land and Wealth.
  • The Upper Class values Academia, Adventure and Glory.
