Changed Spells

Revivify (3rd) / Resurrection (7th) / Wish (9th)

  • After the third revive, the soul becomes more prone to corruption and can forget it's own shape causing deformations such as missing limbs, extra limbs and potentially worse.

    Not only is magic largely illegal in two large countries but also it prevents players from thinking "Nah, I'd resurrect" when charging into battle knowing they have a Cleric.

Goodberry (1st)

  • Provides nourishment for a week instead of a day.

    You're travelling in weeks and this will also cut down the "Tax of Common Sense" (explained further down)

Identify (1st)

  • Only works on objects imbued with magic, anything mechanical and without magic doesn't work and requires appraisals from an Engineer/Artificer.

    NO, WHY ON EARTH WOULD IDENTIFY WORK ON NON-MAGIC SHIT?

Sending (3rd)

  • Changed to not cover other planes.
  • You can allow yourself to be used as a conduit for someone else to send their message to their target of choice, however the Conduit also hears the message both ways.

    Honestly, it creates narrative problems early on to sending across planes and I really can't be bothered with the 5% chance. Contact Other Plane has been bolstered to reflect this as well as the additional usage.

Leomund's Tiny Hut / Tiny Hut (3rd)

  • This is not a spell you can learn through normal means and use of it in the Kingdom of Graodom without a license will grant fines due to Leomund owning this spell. This is locked behind a line of quests for Leomund, in Graodom.

    Due to mechanical changes surrounding how often you can get Long-Rests, a Level 3 spell would invalidate most of it. To add, this also allows an option/route to pursue legally obtaining a license and scroll for it in-game. Yes, I know. Leomund is a dick in world too, that's the idea, he privatized a spell.

Water Breathing (3rd Level)

  • Exclusive to Sorcerers, Druids and Rangers.

    I just don't see any other class using this spell, if anything they'd more than likely use Alter Self for Aquatic Form.

Contact Other Plane (5th)

  • You can alternatively use it to speak with someone you are familiar with (similar to Sending) without the one word limitations, being granted exactly 10 minutes of talking time.

    This helps give a spell that has a lot of potential into something both helpful to those willing to strike bargains and those looking for lost allies.

Simulacrum (7th)

  • A Simulacrum cannot cast Simulacrum, a simulacrum does not have a soul or anything to draw from.

    No infinite bullshit for you random mage!

True Resurrection (9th)

  • Cures afflictions ailing the soul.

    Sounds weird but I'm sure it'll make sense in due time.

Wish (9th)

  • Using it by means of a spell-slot is an exhausting process, you cannot cast Wish again for 24 hours regardless of if you have long-rested during those 24 hours.

    Balancing Wish is like fighting the wind lol, this is just to prevent spam for Level 20+ :KEKL:
