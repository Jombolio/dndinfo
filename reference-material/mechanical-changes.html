Mechanical Changes

Mechanical Changes to Core Rules

No Flanking Rule

  • You can use strategy and roleplay to gain advantages during fights.

    Flanking is boring and lazy, I prefer to reward roleplay and in-game strategy.

Long Rests are Strict

  • You can only Long Rest at Inns / Homes, Long Resting focusses more on mental fatigue and the ability to unwind. When you are on the road/not at an inn or home. However, you can Short Rest even in Dungeons so long as you have 1 hour spare.
  • Long Rests for everyone (including Elves and any species that says otherwise) is 8 hours.

    Due to the nature of the campaign, you're characters will often not feel safe on the road and stay alert. Those "Long Rests" are where you're able to lower your guard without punishment.

Heroic Inspiration, Super Inspirations Dramatic Inspirations

  • Heroic Inspirations more than likely will be handed out like candy, these can be used to roll any d20 (attack roll, saving throw, skill check etc) at advantage. Using it on a Disadvantage Role causes it to become a Neutral Roll (Roll-Once). Using it on an Advantage Role does nothing. These do not stack.
  • Super Inspiration is handed down either because of DM mistakes or Player's feeling shafted by something. This turns any roll into an advantage roll. Using it on a Disadvantage Roll lets you re-roll any roll (Attack Roll, Damage Roll, Encounter Roll, etc) again at Advantage. Using it on an Advantage Roll lets you roll once more at Advantage, you must take the highest option of all four rolls though. These do stack!
  • Dramatic Inspirations are handed once per session and do not roll over to the next session, they contain abilities ranging from the mundane ranging to the game-breaking. You are not allowed to disclose what your Dramatic is to the players until the end of the session to avoid colluding. At the start of the session, you will be asked to roll a 1d100 and a 1d20 for the corresponding Dramatic Inspiration. There are rare occasions that these will stack.

    Heroic Inspirations are pretty standard, Super Inspirations also exist because I am not perfect and will make mistakes. Dramatic Inspirations help provide an element of controlled chaos and can let a player have the ability to completely flip the dynamics in a moments notice.

Rule of Common Sense

  • Your characters aren't brain dead, they will eat and drink when they need to.

    Sure, while those "Feast Moments" are nice, we don't need to play Rust.

Tax of Common Sense

  • Your gear and rations don't maintain themselves, after an indiscriminate amount of time you will need to cough up coin to stock up on rations and maintenance supplies for your gear.

    Mainly an addition to the rule of common sense, while "Shopping Sessions" are nice, spending 2 hours discussing the logistics of travel is rather tedious and can take away from the flow. Think of it as background maintenance.

Tools Re-scale

  • Tools like Thieves' Tool are rolled using the [Associated Ability Roll]+[Modifier]+[Proficiency Bonus] if proficient. The tools are assigned as such:
    Strength: Carpenter's Tools, Cobbler's Tools, Mason's Tools, Smith's Tools, Woodcarver's Tools
    Dexterity: Calligrapher's Supplies, Potter's Tools, Leatherworker's Tools, Painter's Supplies, Weaver's Tools, Thieves' Tools
    Constitution: Glassblower's Tools
    Intelligence: Tinker's Tools, Gaming Sets, Navigator's Tools
    Wisdom: Alchemist's Supplies, Brewer's Supplies, Cartographer's Tools, Cook's Utensils, Jeweller's Tools, Herbalism Kit, Poisoner's Kit, Land Vehicles, Water Vehicles
    Charisma: Disguise Kit, Forgery Kit, Musical Instruments

    I personally think this helps set Thieves and Artificers above others in-terms of tool usage.

Concentration Checks

  • Formula is DC = 10 + Spell Level + (Damage Received / 4)

    Scales better in my personal opinion

Only Gold and Electrum is used

  • No Silver or Copper. Self Explanatory.

    I know what you're thinking "What the fuck is Electrum and why are we using it!?", you'll learn in time in regards to Electrum. Gold is used for largely everything in the Overworld.

Pantheon is Homebrew

There is homebrew Pantheon (with familiar names, no doubt). However, knowledge of some gods is higher than others.

Critical Hit Rules

  • To be decided. The choices are Double Damage Dice or Double Damage.

    We'll more than likely discuss it Session 1 or Session 0.

Exhaustion Re-Work

  • Each Level of Exhaustion instead provides a cumulative -2 to all attack rolls, saving throws and ability checks.
  • Your Spell DC is lowered by the same amount as your exhaustion levels.
  • Your Speed is reduced by 5 X [Exhaustion Level] in feet.
  • You die when this reaches Level 6.
  • A Long Rest alleviated one level of exhaustion.
  • Spending a Week Downtime in a somewhere you own or an inn you rented automatically clears all levels of exhaustion (provided you are able to get consistent long-rests through the week).

    I just don't like remembering all of the effects of the different levels of exhaustion honestly lol

Item Pricing Rework

  • Magic Items use Sane Magical Item Prices as a foundation. Price will also vary dependant on location and vendor.

    This keeps players checking other vendors rather than just relying on the first one they meet. While it more than likely sounds expensive, due to having no Copper or Silver, this will likely be easily attainable.

Heroic Dice Roll

  • Ability Scores are rolled using a Homebrew Method. Roll 24d6 until you get a result higher than 90 but lower than 120, drop the two highest and the two lowest. Then Group them into three's and add them up in their respective groups, those are your scores.

Example

[6, 6, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 4, 4, 4, 4, 4, 3, 3, 3, 3, 3, 2, 2, 2, 1, 1]
becomes
[5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 4, 4, 4, 4, 4, 3, 3, 3, 3, 3, 2]
which then becomes
[15, 15, 13, 12, 9, 8]

You guy's aren't just your average run of the mill adventurers... You're special! You don't know why yet though...
